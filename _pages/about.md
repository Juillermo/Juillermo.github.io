I am an **interdisciplinary researcher** mostly working within the fields of **computational social sciences** using 
tools from complex systems, network science, agent-based simulations, and Artificial Intelligence.
My main focus of research is the study of how external influence can affect the dynamics of opinions within a social 
group, its social norms, or other phenomena such as consensus or opinion fragmentation.
While I have previously focused on theoretical and computational approaches, my current aim is to further integrate my 
expertise with that of researchers from other disciplines to increase the significance and impact of my research.

I have published peer-reviewed articles on various topics, including opinion dynamics, party competition, 
multimorbidity, swarm sensing, voting advice applications, and the interpretability of black-box models.
